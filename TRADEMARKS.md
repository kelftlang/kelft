# Kelft name and Brandmark

Kelft, the Kelft name, and the approved Brandmark identify the Kelft project.
Software and documentation licenses do not grant trademark rights.

You may use the name descriptively to identify Kelft, compatibility, or your
contribution. Do not imply endorsement, sponsorship, or official status.
Community tools must identify their own maintainer. Forks must identify their
modifications and use distinct product branding when distributing a modified
product as their own. Descriptive references to Kelft remain permitted.

Approved brand artwork included with official documentation may be
redistributed unchanged with those artifacts for identification. This is not a
general artwork license. Other promotional uses, merchandise, or altered marks
require separate permission. Preserve asset provenance and applicable attribution;
third-party brand rights remain separate. Software licenses do not cover fonts
or editable wordmarks unless their own terms say so.

The branding source is [kelft-branding](https://github.com/kelftlang/kelft-branding).
Official styling is lowercase `kelft`, with the tagline “Intent, compiled.” Follow
the artwork's clear-space, contrast, and geometry guidance. This policy does not
assert registered trademark status.
